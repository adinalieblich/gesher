SHEET S-02 · THE SYSTEMWHAT IT IS, AND THE RULES IT CANNOT BREAK

Software, not a chatbot.

Is it AI or software? It is software. Gesher is an application you deploy, single-tenant, in your own environment. It uses large language models through an API as one component to do the reading and the drafting. The LLM is the engine under the hood, it is not the product. The product is the pipeline around it: extract every fact with provenance, build the record, flag conflicts, draft with citations, run the citation and review checks, export. That pipeline is deterministic software with rules the model cannot break.

GENERAL NOTES · THREE RULES THE PIPELINE ENFORCES
  1. N1.It never invents a value. Every number comes from a source document or a referenced standard. No source, no sentence.

NOTES · YOUR DATASINGLE TENANT. YOURS.

Single tenant. Yours.

The usual assumption about AI tools is that anything you send is stored overseas and used for training. Gesher is designed the opposite way.

Installed in your environment

A single-tenant system deployed in your servers or your cloud. Your documents and the extracted record stay in your tenant. No shared data plane.

Never used to train an LLM

Content is sent to the model endpoint to be drafted and comes back. Zero-retention terms: not stored by the provider, never used for training.

Auditable end to end

Every fact, source, conflict resolution and engineer decision is logged. The audit trail exports with the project.

SHEET S-06 · THE BUSINESS CASEWHY VERTICAL AI · WHY CIVIL ENGINEERING · WHY NOW

Vertical AI is the category. Civil engineering is the white space.

Vertical AI is software built for one industry's workflow, data and rules, not a general chatbot. It is the fastest-moving category in AI: single-industry companies have reached multi-billion valuations in under two years, because vertical AI does the work, not just the tooling. Gesher is vertical AI for civil engineering reports, the same playbook that built billion-dollar legal and medical AI, applied to the engineering report.

EXHIBIT 4 · THE MARKET THESISWHY THE CEILING IS HIGH

NEA: vertical SaaS competes for software spend (~1% of US GDP). Vertical AI competes for services spend (~13% of US GDP), because it does the work, not just the tooling.

Sequoia: ~US$10 trillion of services that software never reached. "US legal services alone are $400B, equal to the entire software market." McKinsey: generative AI could add US$2.6 to 4.4 trillion a year. The labour budget is the new software budget.

For Gesher: a report is a service deliverable, so the budget we attack is the engineer's time, not a software licence.
